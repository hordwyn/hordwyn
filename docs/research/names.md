Date checked: 2026-11-12 (every lookup in this file).
Scope: research only. Nothing was registered, no registrar and no person was contacted, no
code was changed.
Read first: docs/research/domains.md (method, the collision, prices). This file repeats that
method for other names.

Owner input used: "it's sad. I liked that kith had a meaning like that", and later the same

ash as "ae", long vowels without marks. The dictionary headword is given in the same
transliteration.

## 2. Method and limits

Same tools as domains.md section 2 and 3:

- Registration: RDAP at the registry (`rdap.publicinterestregistry.org` for .org,
  `rdap.verisign.com` for .com and .net, `pubapi.registry.google` for .dev and .app,
  `rdap.denic.de` for .de), port-43 WHOIS at `whois.eu` for .eu, and NS lookups through
  DNS over HTTPS (https://dns.google/resolve). "Free" below means RDAP 404 (or WHOIS
  "AVAILABLE") and NXDOMAIN. It does not show a premium price or a registry block; check at
  the registrar right before buying.
- Namespaces: GitHub user/org and repository-name search (api.github.com), crates.io, npm
  (name and `@name` scope), Docker Hub user and org, PyPI, Codeberg, GitLab group search.
- App stores: iTunes Search API, US and DE storefronts
  (https://itunes.apple.com/search?term=NAME&entity=software, and `&country=de`). The API
  matches loosely, so unrelated titles come back; only same-or-similar names count. The DE
  query was confirmed to work (it returns "KithKeep: Stay Close" for "kithkeep"). Google
  Play search pages answer HTTP 200 but are script-rendered, so the script cannot read hits:
  **Google Play is "not checked by script" for every candidate; check by hand.**
- Web search: one or two searches per finalist.
- Meanings: a dictionary entry per word, quoted in section 4 and 5. Could not be reached:
  etymonline (HTTP 403), the Bosworth-Toller site search (script-rendered), Wikipedia has no
  article "Samanunga worto" under that title. Substitutes are named where used.
- Trade marks: could not be queried by script, again. TMview answered "Request Rejected",
  EUIPO eSearch gave no answer, DPMAregister returned the search form without a result
  list, USPTO returned the application shell. Manual searches are listed in section 8.

## 5. Top 3 in detail

### 5.1 samanunga

- Meaning: Old High German `samanunga` (feminine noun), from `samanon` "to gather" (modern
  German "sammeln", "zusammen", "Sammlung" are the same family). Proto-West Germanic
  `*samnungu` "gathering, assembly", with the descendants Old High German "samanunga", Old
  Dutch "samanunga" (gloss: "gathering"), Old Saxon "samnunga", Old English "samnung"
  (Wiktionary data via kaikki.org; Wiktionary entry "samanunga", Old Dutch section, citing
  the Oudnederlands Woordenboek 2012). The Leipzig etymological dictionary of Old High
  German (EWA) glosses it as collection, (monastic) community, congregation; that page
  could not be fetched directly (404 to the script), the gloss is from a search result
  summary of https://ewa.saw-leipzig.de/articles/samanung/de. **Owner: open that page once
  in a browser.**
- The book: "Samanunga worto" ("collection of words") is a Latin-Old High German glossary
  from about 790 to 800, a reworking of the Abrogans, which counts as the oldest German
  book; main manuscript Vienna, Austrian National Library, Cod. 162. Its opening line
  pairs Latin "Congregationes" with "samanunga" (search result summary of a dbnl.org
  scholarly introduction; Koebler's Old High German dictionary lists the work as
  "Samanunga (Wien, Oesterreichische Nationalbibliothek Cod. 162)").
- Why it fits: one word for both halves of the product: the people who gather (game
  group, "watched with") and the thing gathered (a collection, a catalogue). The hub, an
  open catalogue, has a 1200-year-old namesake that was exactly that: a shared word list.
- Pronunciation: sa-ma-NOON-ga; German "Samanunga" as written. Spelling after hearing: as
  spoken in both languages; the only trap is "-nunga" versus "-nunger".
- Other meanings: none found in English or German. Not checked beyond that; "Nunga" is a
  self-name of Aboriginal people in South Australia (general knowledge, not looked up
  today), which is not an insult but worth knowing.
- Collisions: none (section 4).
- Domains free: .org .com .net .de .eu .dev .app .at .ch .io.
- Cost: about 47 to 64 per year for .org .com .de .eu .dev.
- Verdict: best meaning for this product, completely unclaimed, a little long.

### 5.2 skemtan

- Meaning: Old Norse `skemtan` (also `skemtun`), feminine: "an entertainment"; "hafa s. af
  e-u, to amuse oneself with a thing"; "skemtanar-ganga, a pleasure-walk"
  (Cleasby-Vigfusson, entry "skemtan"). The verb `skemta`: "[prop. from skamr = to
  shorten]: to amuse, entertain ... skemta ser, to amuse oneself, play" (same dictionary),
  so literally "making the time short", the same picture as German "Kurzweil".
  `sagna-skemtan` (story-entertainment) has its own entry pointing to "saga": stories told
  aloud to entertain the hall.
- Why it fits: it names what is tracked (what you watch, read, hear and play to pass the
  time) and, through sagna-skemtan, doing it in company. It does not mean "one's people";
  that half of "kith" is implied, not stated.
- Pronunciation: SKEM-tan in English and German. Spelling after hearing: mostly right;
  possible slips "skemten", "scemtan".
- Other meanings: Icelandic "skemmtun" and, by general knowledge, Faroese "skemtan" still
  mean entertainment or fun (the Faroese form was not confirmed in a dictionary today);
  Swedish "skaemt" and Danish "skaemt" mean a joke. All friendly. English ears may hear a
  faint "scheme" or "scam" in the first syllable; judge by saying it aloud.
- Collisions: none. Skemman (skemman.is, Icelandic thesis repository) is a different word.
- Domains free: .org .com .net .de .eu .dev .app .io, and .fo .is .no .dk .se show no
  delegation.
- Cost: about 47 to 64 per year for .org .com .de .eu .dev.
- Verdict: the most practical name on the list; meaning one step further from "kith".

### 5.3 couthie

- Meaning: Scots `couthie` (also coothie, cuthie), adjective. Of persons: "Agreeable,
  sociable, friendly, sympathetic, pleasant." Of things or places: "Comfortable, snug,
  neat"; "Pleasant, agreeable." Etymology note: compare Middle English `cuthi`, "known,
  familiar"; see "Couth" (Scottish National Dictionary at dsl.ac.uk). "Kith" is the noun
  from the same root: Old English `cyththu` "kinship, kinsfolk, relations", from
  Proto-Germanic `*kunthitho` "knowledge, acquaintance"; "More at couth" (Wiktionary,
  "kith"). So couthie is the adjective for how it feels among one's kith.
- Pronunciation: KOO-thee (th as in "thin"). Spelling after hearing: uncertain even in
  English ("coothie", "couthy"); in German the "th" is lost, as with "kith".
- Other meanings: none negative. Sense 3 in the dictionary, marked obsolete: with a
  negative, "uncanny".
- Collisions: Couthie, a Scottish gift and souvenir brand (couthie.co.uk, a Shopify shop;
  Edinburgh Chamber of Commerce listing); Couthie Cafe Ltd, Thurso (Companies House
  SC808085). Different goods, and an ordinary Scots dictionary word, so expect more small
  users and a weaker mark. No app, no repository, no package.
- Domains: .org .net .de .eu .dev .app .scot .io free. **.com taken** since 2007,
  redirects to a domain reseller's sale page.
- Cost: about 35 to 48 per year for .org .de .eu .dev; the .com would be a one-off
  purchase at the reseller's price (not checked).
- Verdict: the closest in spirit and root to "kith"; loses on .com, spelling and German.

### 5.4 Comparison: keep kithkeep

- Meaning: kith = "friends, acquaintances, kinsfolk" (section 5.3 source) + keep.
- Collision, stated precisely: (1) the iOS App Store app "KithKeep: Stay Close" (bundle
  `com.sanva.kithkeep`, released 2026-10-06, listed in the US and DE storefronts; confirmed
  again today through the iTunes Search API); (2) `kithkeep.com`, registered 2026-01-12;
  (3) `kithkeep.app`, registered 2026-05-17, parked. **Not on Google Play**: the owner
  checked by hand and sees no KithKeep there; the earlier script check found none either.
  Plus one dormant GitHub repository (`andryous/kithkeep-project`). Details: domains.md
  section 3.
- Domains free: .org .net .de .eu .dev .io.
- Cost: `kithkeep.org` 12 to 15, with .de .eu .dev about 35 to 48 per year (domains.md
  section 8: 45 to 60 including the hub domain).
- Verdict: usable on .org today; the costs are not money but position: later user of the
  name in the same class of goods, no .com, no .app, no App Store name, shared search
  results, and a naming root ("kith") that a dozen other projects picked up this year.

## 6. What a rename touches in this repository

The codename was kept out of the code on purpose (rule 9), so the list is short. For
package v1-release:

1. `config/brand/default/brand.toml`: `name` and `short_name` (lines 2 and 3). The only
   runtime occurrence.
2. `src/rust/xtask/src/whitelabel.rs`: `codename()` returns `["kith", "keep"].concat()`.
   Change the two halves to the new name (still split, so the file does not match itself).
   Until this is changed the guard keeps looking for the old word and would not catch the
   new one in code.
3. `README.md`: title (line 1) and the codename sentence (line 5).
4. Docs that state the name as a rule or default: `docs/HANDOFF.md` (rule 9 text at line
   182, Q8 default brand at lines 316-317, the `glab ... -R` path at line 1013),
   `docs/design/DESIGN.md` (title, line 469), `docs/design/KICKOFF.md` (5 places),
   `docs/handoff/agent-briefs/rules.md`, `docs/handoff/fable-briefs/common.md`,
   `PKG-implement.md`, `F9-record-owner-answers.md`. Past reviews under `docs/reviews/` and
   `docs/research/domains.md` are records; leave them and add one decision entry in
   `docs/DECISIONS.md` (it has no occurrence today).
5. GitLab path: `hordwyn/hordwyn` (remote `origin`). Renaming the
   project path changes the clone URL and the CI/registry path; GitLab redirects the old
   path, but update the remote, the `glab -R` references and any Pages URL.
6. Local checkout directory `<repo>`: appears in briefs
   (`rules.md`, `common.md`), in the memory path of the coding assistant, in the systemd
   unit name mentioned in HANDOFF line 967, and in `.dev/` runtime files (regenerated).
   Optional; renaming the directory is cosmetic.
7. Not affected, checked: crate and package names (all neutral: `app`, `server`, `cli`,

8. Outside the repo, on the day of the decision: buy the domains, reserve the namespaces
   (GitHub org, GitLab group, Codeberg, Docker Hub, crates.io name, npm scope), as listed
   in domains.md section 3.2 and 8.

## 7. Recommendation

Rename before v1, to **samanunga**.

- It answers the owner's wish directly: a real word from an old language (Old High
  German), with a meaning that says "the people gathered" and "the collection" at once,
  and a namesake that is one of the oldest books in German.
- It is the cleanest name checked: .org, .com, .de, .eu, .dev, .net, .app free, no app, no
  package, no organisation, no product.
- Cost of the rename today: section 6, about an hour of edits. Cost after v1: a migration
  for every installed instance's documentation, image name and links.

Second choice **skemtan** if the name must be short; third **couthie** only if staying
close to "kith" matters more than .com and German spelling. If the owner would rather keep
"kith" in the name after all, **kithshelf** is the one clean candidate (section 3a), with
the caveats there.

Keeping kithkeep on `.org` remains possible; it is the weakest of the five on every
practical point and the strongest only on attachment.

Before buying anything: section 8.
