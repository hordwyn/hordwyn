## Must fix before commit

### OT2C-1 (medium) - the hero sentence disagrees with the hero figure about half of the time

- Where: `src/rust/crates/web/src/time_tiles.rs:67-82` (`hero_sentence`) against
  `src/rust/crates/web/src/charts.rs` `hours_of` (the figure).
- Problem: the figure rounds to the nearest hour from ten hours on (`hours_of`:
  `(minutes / 60.0).round()`), the sentence floors (`let hours = minutes / 60`) and derives
  days and hours from that. Whenever the minutes past the hour are 30 or more, the two
  lines of the same tile differ by one hour.
- Failure scenario: 2,870 minutes banked (47 h 50 min). The tile reads "48 hours" and under
  it "That is 1 day and 23 hours of movies, shows, listening and play." With the unit
  test's library plus 30 minutes (79,530 minutes): "1,326 hours" above "That is 55 days and
  5 hours" (= 1,325). The owner asked for this tile as the primary figure; 55 x 24 + 5 is
  the first thing a reader checks. The existing tests only use totals on the full hour
  (79,500 and 1,500 minutes), so they cannot see it.
- Also in the same function: a full day reads "That is 2 days and 0 hours of ..." (2,880
  minutes), and from 23 h 30 min to 23 h 59 min the figure says "24 hours" while the
  sentence is still the under-a-day one (harmless, but the same cause).
- Suggested fix: take the hours of the sentence from the shown figure (`hours_of(minutes)`
  as a whole number once it is 10 or more), so both lines use one rounding; choose the
  sentence by that number too (>= 24). Leave the hours part out when it is 0 ("That is 2
  days of ...", one more Fluent variant in en and de). Add unit cases for 2,870 and 2,880
  minutes.

## May defer

### OT2C-2 (medium) - activity that has neither time nor pages has no place on Home any more

- Where: `src/rust/crates/web/src/time_tiles.rs:85-111` (`also_line` lists a kind only for
  minutes > 0 or pages > 0), `src/rust/crates/web/src/home.rs` `HomeStats` (the per-group
  amounts list `totals-list-*` is gone).
- Problem: before, Home showed each group's counts ("Watch: 12 watched, 500 episodes").
  Now a count is shown only inside the four tiles (movie, show, book, video and board
  game). For every other kind a count appears nowhere on Home, and its time or pages only
  when not zero.
- Failure scenario: a user who tracks anime (or podcasts) imported without
  `episode_runtime_minutes` - the normal case per DEC-211 and ux-owner-test.md 4.3 - with
  500 logged episodes. Home shows "0 hours", "Nothing with a known length logged yet.",
  four tiles at zero, no "Also" line, and "Watch: 40 entries, ...". The 500 episodes that
  Home showed until HEAD are not on the page. Same for music or audiobooks logged without
  a position, and for visual novels with plays but no duration (their plays are not in the
  Games tile either).
- Why defer is acceptable: the design (4.2) says "only when not zero" for the "Also" list
  and the Stats page tabs still carry the counts. It is a loss against HEAD that the
  design did not weigh, so it wants an owner word or a DEC rather than a silent default.
- Suggested fix: in the "Also" line, a kind without time and pages falls back to its
  leading count ("Anime 500 episodes", "Visual novel 12 plays"); or record in DEC-574 that
  such kinds are intentionally absent from Home until ot2g.

### OT2C-3 (low) - figures of one or two minutes read "0 hours", and the tiles need not add up to the hero as shown

- Where: `charts.rs` `hours_of`; `time_tiles.rs:158-170`; `stats_extras.rs` `compare_lines`
  (`find(|l| l.this > 0 || l.last > 0)` on raw minutes) and `CompareList`.
- Problem and scenarios:
  (a) `hours_of(2)` is 0.0. A user whose only timed log is 2 minutes of music sees the hero
  "0 hours" with the non-zero sentence "Movies, shows, listening and play, added up.", the
  line "Also: Music 0 hours", and on the All tab the year comparison shows "Listen: Hours - 0 in 2026, 0 in
  2025" instead of falling back to the group's first count.
  (b) DEC-572 says tiles, group rows and hero "add up exactly". That holds in minutes, not
  in what is printed: movies 10 h 25 min, shows 10 h 25 min, games 10 h 25 min print as
  "10 hours" three times under a hero of "31 hours".
- Suggested fix: (a) treat a time amount as "something" only when it shows as at least 0.1
  hours (one helper used by `also_line`, `compare_lines` and the hero sentence), or show
  "under 0.1 hours"; (b) no code change, but reword DEC-572 ("add up in minutes; shown
  hours are rounded per figure").

### OT2C-4 (low) - the totals row and the chart of the same tab can differ by the rounding rule

- Where: `src/rust/crates/app/src/stats.rs:296-306` (minutes per kind, all time) against
  `:360-363` (`stats_series`: minutes per bucket over the whole group) and
  `src/rust/crates/app/src/goals.rs:66`, `:280` (per group or goal window).
- Problem: three different places floor seconds to minutes. DEC-572 states this and argues
  the figures are not shown side by side; on the Stats page they are: the totals row
  "Hours" sits directly above the "Hours" chart, whose caption shows the sum of its buckets.
  Also `stats_series(None, ..)` counts kinds of the group Other that have no library entry,
  which `stats_totals` leaves out.
- Failure scenario: Listen tab, year view, all activity in one year: an audiobook with
  35,790 s and music with 45 s. Totals row: 596 + 0 = 596 minutes = "9.9"; chart caption:
  35,835 s = 597 minutes = "10". Only the timed listening kinds carry loose seconds, so the
  gap is at most a few minutes and visible only at a rounding edge. The gap existed before
  the package (per group against per bucket) and was then visible in minutes; the package
  did not make it worse.
- Suggested fix (ot2f or ot2g, same files): carry seconds to the edge of the app layer and
  floor once per shown figure, or floor per kind and bucket everywhere.

### OT2C-5 (low) - a library without runtimes gets an empty "Hours" chart first in every tab but Read

- Where: `src/rust/crates/web/src/charts.rs` `measures_for` (Hours first, always),
  `home.rs` `SeriesCharts` and `TotalsLine`.
- Failure scenario: a library imported through generic JSONL (no `runtime_minutes`), 164
  movies watched. Watch tab: the first tile is "Hours 0" and the first chart is "Hours in
  Watch, ...: none yet" (an empty plot), the charts with data follow. `compare_lines` got a
  fallback for exactly this case on the All tab; the tab's own row and charts did not.
  The footnote "Titles without a known length count as 0." is on the page, so this is
  explainable, not wrong.
- Suggested fix: order a tab's measures so that those with any amount come first (time
  still leads when it has any), or collapse an all-zero chart to its sentence.

### OT2C-6 (low) - "Also: Manga One page"

- Where: `time_tiles.rs:95-107` uses `Measure::Pages.counted`, whose key
  `stats-count-pages` has the stand-alone form "[one] One page" (en.ftl:716-719; de the
  same pattern), inside the sentence `stats-also-part`.
- Failure scenario: exactly one page logged on a manga or comic: "Also: Manga One page"

- Suggested fix: build the part from `measure.text` plus a lower-case unit key, or give
  `stats-count-pages` a numeric one-form as `stats-count-hours` has.

## Checked and found in order

- Per-kind totals (`app/src/stats.rs`): seconds are floored per kind, the group is the sum
  of its kinds, `overall` the sum of the groups; the hero (`overall.amounts.minutes`) and
  the sum of all `kinds` are the same number of minutes. Kinds of the group Other without
  entries are left out of both. The app test checks sorted order, the 90 s + 45 s case,
  an untimed movie and a never-logged kind; it can fail (the log shows 367 against 366).
- Which kind feeds which tile: movie, show, book, video_game + board_game; every other
  kind (anime, podcast, audiobook, music, manga, comic, visual_novel, custom kinds) goes to
  "Also" by time and by pages. Progress types are validated against the kind's model, so a
  book cannot carry seconds nor a game pages: no time is in the hero that no line shows.
- Zero states: four tiles always, "0 hours" / "0 pages", own hero sentence, reading note
  only with pages; a library without runtimes keeps its lines in the year comparison.
- DTO: `kinds` is additive with `serde(default)`, no `deny_unknown_fields`; an old answer
  parses to no kinds (contracts test). Web and server ship as one binary.
- Hours: one decimal below ten, whole from ten, through the bundle's locale formatter:
  "1 hour", "1.5 hours", "1,284 hours", "1 Stunde", "1,5 Stunden", "1.284 Stunden" (unit
  tests name each). Plural selection on 1.0 gives the singular.
- R-04: no pace, rate or target wording in the new keys (read in en and de).
- Text equivalents: the hero is a labelled section with a heading (h3 on Home, h2 on
  Stats), the icon is `aria-hidden`, the tiles are a `dl` with `div` groups, "Also" and the
  notes are sentences; the hours chart keeps its summary as the name and its table (now in
  hours, as the chart); the status bars keep their sentence plus the new line. No new
  interactive element except the native disclosure; classes used are existing tokens
  (`text-muted-foreground`, `text-display` in `src/web/style/input.css:141`).
- Fluent: the same 19 new keys in en and de, `stats-entries` removed from both; en is
  ASCII; non-ASCII in Rust tests is written as escapes. No brand name in code. DEC-572..574
  have the six fields. No function over 100 lines (`Stats` 67 after the split).
- Out of scope: the split of `Stats()` into `stats_url_state()` is the only change not
  asked for; it was needed for the line limit and moves code unchanged.
- The axe test at 360 px: accepted. The first version resized a page that had been
  scrolled at 1280 px, which left the tab strip half under the fixed bottom bar; that is a
  scroll position, not a layout the app forces. `main` has bottom padding of the bar's
  height (`shell.rs:358`) and the page sets `scroll-padding-bottom` to it
  (`input.css:181`), so a user or a focus move can always bring a control clear of the
  bar. Loading at 360 px is what the other specs do. The new block does push the tabs
  about one screen down on a phone; that is the design (4.2), not a defect.
- Goals keep the measure "Minutes" next to "Hours" everywhere else: declared out of scope
  in DEC-573 and the log; converting would change stored targets. No finding.
