# Review of package ot2d (colour and page widths), 2026-11-11

Reviewed: the uncommitted working tree against HEAD (c1de777): 32 changed files, 2 new
files (`crates/ui/src/tint.rs`, `crates/web/src/tint.rs`), DEC-580..583,
`docs/fable/round2/ot2d-progress.md`, the 20 screenshots in `docs/fable/round2/ot2d-shots/`.

Spec: `docs/fable/round2/next-packages.md` section "ot2d", `ux-owner-test.md` section 3,
DEC-540, DEC-541, DEC-496, OT-13 / OT-14, HANDOFF section 2 (rule 9) and 4,
`docs/handoff/agent-briefs/rules.md`.

Method and limits: read-only. No cargo, npm or Playwright was run by the reviewer; the test
results quoted are those of the progress log. Contrast ratios below were recomputed by hand
(WCAG 2.2 relative luminance, alpha blended in sRGB, 8-bit rounding) from the hex values in
`config/brand/default/brand.toml` and the zinc values the brand crate mirrors. Widths in
OT2D-1 are measured in the screenshots and added up from the Tailwind classes; they were not
measured in a browser. That is said again at the finding.

## Must fix before commit

### OT2D-1 (medium) The group marker dots make the palette's scope switcher wider than a phone

- Where: `src/rust/crates/web/src/palette.rs:693` (`group_option` for the scopes) and
  `:706` (`<Segmented id="palette-scope" ... class="mb-3"/>`, no scroll wrapper);
  `src/rust/crates/ui/src/chip.rs:209` (the dot, `size-2 shrink-0`, plus `gap-1.5`).
- Problem: each dot adds 8 px + 6 px to an option, 56 px for the four groups. The switcher
  "All / Watch / Read / Listen / Play" is now 358 px wide in English (measured in
  `library-1280-light.png` and `stats-1280-light.png`: track from x = 16 to x = 374 where
  nothing constrains it); before the package it was 302 px. Library, History and Stats put
  the switcher in a `-mx-4 max-w-[calc(100%+2rem)] overflow-x-auto px-4` wrapper
  (`library.rs:329`, `history.rs:441`, `home.rs:554`), the palette does not. On phones the
  palette is a full-screen dialog with `p-6` (`ui/src/dialog.rs:98`,
  `palette.rs:236`), so its content box is viewport - 48 px.
- Failure scenario: a 360 px phone, open search (the "Add" tab or the magnifier). Content
  box 312 px, switcher 358 px: the `inline-flex` track cannot shrink (single words, dot is
  `shrink-0`), the dialog's implicit grid column grows to 358 px, so the search field and
  the result list stretch with it and end 22 px beyond the right screen edge; the dialog
  (`overflow-y-auto` on phones) now also scrolls sideways. That is two-dimensional scrolling
  at 360 px (WCAG 1.4.10) in the central add flow. At 390 px the overflow is 16 px: nothing
  is cut, but the right padding shrinks from 24 px to 8 px. Before the package 302 px fit
  into 312 px. German ("Alle / Sehen / Lesen / Hoeren / Spielen") is a little wider still.
  Not run in a browser by the reviewer; the numbers come from the screenshots and the
  classes, please confirm at 360 px.
- Why the tests did not see it: `expectReflow` (`src/web/tests/helpers.ts:50`) compares
  `documentElement.scrollWidth` with `clientWidth`; overflow inside a `position: fixed`
  dialog does not change either. The palette test at 360 px (`sources.spec.ts:46-63`)
  therefore cannot fail on this.
- Side effect on the wrapped switchers: at 360 px the Library / History / Stats switcher
  (358 px in 328 px) now scrolls inside its strip, "Play" is half hidden with no hint that
  the strip scrolls. Before it fit. Acceptable, but worth one look.
- Suggested fix: give the palette's switcher the same scroll wrapper as the other three, or
  (smaller) hide the dot below 26 rem (`max-[26rem]:hidden` on the dot) or drop to `px-2`
  per segment when a tint is set. Add an assertion that can fail: in the 360 px palette
  test compare `scrollWidth` and `clientWidth` of the `role="dialog"` element, and extend
  `expectReflow` to do that for any open dialog.

### OT2D-2 (medium) Lists and People are "grid pages" by name only: the content stays at 64 / 56 rem

- Where: `src/rust/crates/web/src/lists.rs:68` (`<div class="grid max-w-5xl gap-4">`),
  `src/rust/crates/web/src/people.rs:63` (`<div class="grid max-w-4xl gap-4">`);
  `src/rust/crates/web/src/shell.rs:58-63` (`page_width`); test
  `src/web/tests/shell.spec.ts:328-362`; DEC-582.
- Problem: `page_width` makes `main` 96 rem wide on `/lists` and `/people`, but the whole
  page content sits in a wrapper capped at 1024 px (Lists) or 896 px (People), without
  `mx-auto`, so it is flush left in the wider column. The card grids inside stop at three
  (`lists.rs:120`, `lg:grid-cols-3`) and two columns (`people.rs:209`, `md:grid-cols-2`).
  DEC-541 / ux-owner-test 3.2 say "more columns" on these pages.
- Failure scenario: 2560 px screen, `/people`. `main` spans x = 512 .. 2048, the cards end
  at x = 1424: 608 px of the column are empty on the right and the content's centre is
  304 px left of the screen's centre. With the 80 rem column of DEC-496 it was 176 px. The
  owner's finding OT-14 ("much room left and right") is unchanged on Lists and People and
  the page now looks more lopsided than before. The top bar (1536 px) makes the gap
  obvious.
- Why the test did not see it: it measures the bounding box of `main#main` and of
  `header > div`, never a content element. With the inner cap in place it still reports
  1536 px.
- Suggested fix: remove the two caps (keep `max-w-prose` on the lead text if wanted) and
  let the grids grow (`xl:grid-cols-4` for list cards, `xl:grid-cols-3` for person cards),
  or, if the cards should not grow, make `/lists` and `/people` record pages in
  `page_width` and say so in DEC-582. Either way let the test measure content: the right
  edge of the card list (`ul[aria-label]`) or of the poster grid against the right edge of
  `main` minus the gutter, for all five grid pages.

### OT2D-3 (medium) The colour guard catches six palette names and nothing else

- Where: `src/rust/xtask/src/whitelabel.rs:106` (`BRAND_PALETTES`), `:109`
  (`COLOUR_FREE_DIRS`), `:117` (`names_brand_palette`); test
  `src/rust/xtask/tests/whitelabel.rs:15`; DEC-583.
- Problem: the test is called `brand_colours_not_hard_coded` and DEC-583 presents it as
  what keeps rule 9 ("no ... colour ... hard-coded outside the default brand config") true.
  It only finds `<family>-<digit>` for violet, fuchsia, orange, teal, blue and purple. It
  does not find:
  - arbitrary values: `bg-[#7008e7]`, `text-[oklch(0.5_0.2_290)]`, `fill-[rgb(112_8_231)]`;
  - inline styles and SVG attributes in Rust: `style="color:#a800b7"`, `fill="#1447e6"`,
    `stroke="rgb(0 120 111)"`, `style:background-color="..."`;
  - every other Tailwind family: `indigo-600`, `pink-500`, `rose-600`, `cyan-600`,
    `green-600`, `lime-500`, `yellow-400`, `slate-700`, and also `zinc-*`, `red-*`,
    `emerald-*`, `amber-*`, `sky-*` in component code (they are meant to be allowed in the
    stylesheet as fallbacks, but `text-emerald-600` in `crates/web/src` bypasses the
    `success` token just the same);
  - anything in `src/rust/apps/server/src` (HTML shell, error pages, mail).
- Failure scenario: a later package adds `class="bg-[#a684ff]"` to a tile, or
  `fill="#7008e7"` to a chart `rect`, or `text-indigo-600` to a link. The sample brand
  (green) then shows a violet element, rule 9 is broken, and
  `brand_colours_not_hard_coded` stays green. The unit test
  `palette_classes_and_variables_are_found_and_tokens_are_not` documents the gap rather
  than closing it (it never feeds an arbitrary value or an attribute).
- Present state: no such literal exists today. A search of `crates/ui/src`,
  `crates/web/src`, `apps/server/src` and `crates/api/src` for any Tailwind family with a
  shade, `[#`, hex, `rgb(`, `oklch(`, `hsl(`, `style="` and colour attributes finds only
  `stroke="currentColor"` / `fill="none"` in `ui/src/icon.rs:25-26` and `bg-black/60` in
  `ui/src/dialog.rs:175`. So a strict guard passes on the tree as it is.
- Suggested fix: two rules instead of one. (a) In the Rust directories (`crates/ui/src`,
  `crates/web/src`, `apps/server/src`): no Tailwind family name followed by `-<digit>` for
  the full family list, no `-[#`, no `#` followed by 3, 6 or 8 hex digits inside a string
  literal, no `rgb(`, `rgba(`, `hsl(`, `oklch(`, `oklab(` (allow-list: the one log message
  in `apps/server/src/lib.rs:554` and the scrim, or move the scrim to a token). (b) In
  `src/web/style`: the six brand families as now. Extend the unit test with one case per
  class of literal (`bg-[#7008e7]`, `fill="#1447e6"`, `style="color: rgb(1 2 3)"`,
  `text-indigo-600`).

### OT2D-4 (medium) The current item of the mobile tab bar is now told apart by hue and weight only

- Where: `src/rust/crates/ui/src/nav.rs:142-144` (`TAB`:
  `aria-[current=page]:font-semibold aria-[current=page]:text-primary`).
- Problem: the top bar and the side navigation mark the current item with a surface
  (`bg-primary-soft`) as well as the accent text. The tab bar only changes the text and icon
  colour and the weight of a 12 px label. The luminance contrast between the current item
  (`primary`) and the others (`muted-foreground`) is 1.06:1 in light (#7008e7 against
  #52525c) and 1.09:1 in dark (#a684ff against #9f9fa9). Before the package it was
  `foreground` against `muted-foreground`, 2.6:1 in light.
- Failure scenario: a user who cannot tell violet from grey (achromatopsia, a greyscale
  display setting, strong sunlight on a phone) looks at the tab bar in `home-390-light.png`:
  "Home" and "Library" differ only by semibold at 12 px. WCAG 1.4.1 is met formally by the
  weight and by `aria-current`, but the visible cue became much weaker than it was, on the
  one navigation phones have.
- Suggested fix: a cue that is not colour, as on the other two navigations: a pill behind
  the icon on `primary-soft`, or a 2 px bar at the
  top of the tab in `primary`. Then the pair is covered by the start-up check as well.

## Checked without a finding

- White-label, present state: no colour literal or palette class in `crates/ui/src`,
  `crates/web/src`, `apps/server/src`, `crates/api/src` (see OT2D-3 for what was searched).
  The stylesheet adds only `var(--secondary)`, `var(--muted)`, `var(--muted-foreground)`
  fallbacks. The zinc hex values in `crates/brand/src/contrast.rs` are the mirror of the
  stylesheet's neutral fallbacks (OT2D-8). No "kithkeep" string added to code.
- Brand scoping: `[theme]` goes to `:root:not(.dark)`, `[theme_dark]` to `:root.dark`
  (`crates/brand/src/lib.rs:171-172`), so a light-only brand keeps the neutral dark theme,
  and the repeated fallbacks in `.dark` keep a nested `.dark` region from inheriting light
  tints. No nested `.dark` wrapper exists in `ui` or `web` today.
- Contrast, default brand, recomputed: the 31 pairs per theme of DEC-580 match (lowest
  text pair orange-700 on orange-50, 4.92). Beyond the check: focus ring (`ring`) on the
  soft surfaces 4.40 to 4.63 light, 5.53 to 5.97 dark; field border (`input`) on the soft
  surfaces 4.40+ light, 3.00 to 3.24 dark (3:1 needed; teal-950 is exactly at 3.00, no
  control sits on a group surface today); accent text of the selected segment and tab on
  the `background` chip 7.30 / 6.98; current navigation item 6.65 / 5.34; chart bars on
  `background` / `card` 5.22+ light and 5.60+ dark, on the `muted` track 4.75+ / 5.22+
  (1.4.11); filled button on the "next up" row 6.65 / 5.34 against the row; tooltip
  (`foreground` / `background`) 19.9 / 19.1. Disabled controls (`opacity-50`) are 2.77 /
  2.50, exempt under 1.4.3 as inactive components and unchanged in mechanism.
- Hover on the current navigation item: `aria-[current=page]` sorts after `hover` in
