## The task: research before any design or code (owner, 2026-11-20)

"It should be properly researched what makes an edition an edition." Do not build until the

### 1. Research (write `docs/research/book-editions.md`)

Licence check first for every source (KICKOFF: no GPL code; reading public API docs and terms
of GPL services is fine, DEC-169). Cite every source with URL and date read. Questions:

- **The models.** What separates work, expression, manifestation and item in IFLA LRM (and
  FRBR before it), and where an "edition" sits; how RDA and library catalogues (MARC 250
  edition statement) use it. What makes two copies the same edition and what makes a new one:
  publisher, format (hardcover, paperback, ebook, audiobook), translation, revised text,
  abridgement, illustrations, printing vs edition, reissue with a new cover, ISBN changes.
- **ISBN rules.** ISBN per format and per edition (ISBN agency rules, International ISBN
  Agency user manual): when a new ISBN is required, when not; ISBN-10 vs ISBN-13; books
  without an ISBN (old books, self-published).
- **How the sources model it.** Open Library (work vs edition records, `/works/{id}/editions.json`,
  edition fields: publishers, publish_date, physical_format, number_of_pages, pagination,
  languages, isbn_10/13, translation_of; already used in 1.0.1 for the year rule, DEC-859);
  Wikidata (literary work vs version, edition, or translation, Q3331189; properties such as
  P629 edition or translation of, P747 has edition, P212 ISBN-13, P1104 number of pages);
  BookBrainz (its edition and edition group model; licence of data and API); and how other
  trackers present it to readers (Goodreads, StoryGraph, Hardcover, Libib, Calibre): what a
  person picks, what they see, how they switch editions mid-read. Terms of each.
- **What it changes for a reader.** Page count and pagination (the progress model counts
  pages: DEC on page totals, `user` provenance, DEC-733..758), audiobook vs print (minutes vs
  pages: is an audiobook an edition of the book or another kind, the `audiobook` kind
  exists), the language of the edition vs the work title (OT-34), cover per edition, year
  (first publication vs this edition, DEC-859).
- **The owner's cases** (OT-26, docs/handoff/owner-test-2026-11-15.md and v2-notes.md):
  want to read a book, later read a special edition; an ISBN search shows that edition; a
  title search lets a person pick; the edition is optional and changeable on the entry;
  existing entries keep working (migration). Ask the owner which book they started on
  2026-11-20 and use it as the worked example.
