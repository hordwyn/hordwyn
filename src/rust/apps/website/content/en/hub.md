## Why a hub

A tracker is only as good as its catalogue. For movies, shows, books and video games there are services that an instance can ask. For board games, and for several other kinds, there is no source that is both complete and open to self-hosted software. That is the gap the hub is meant to close.

## What is planned

- **A shared catalogue at [{hub}]({hub}).** Instances look titles up there and can send back what their members add or correct.
- **Open licences.** Facts on the hub are to be published under CC0 1.0, so anyone may reuse them. Pictures only under CC BY 4.0 or CC0.
- **Board games first.** The other kinds follow; movies and shows come last, because they are served well today.
- **Careful start.** At launch, accounts are by invitation and instances are admitted by an administrator. Contributions are attributed to the instance; a personal hub account is optional.
- **Moderation and a notice-and-action process** from the first day.

## What it is not

- It is not required. An instance works without the hub, with the data sources it has today.
- It is not a place where your library goes. The hub holds catalogue facts, not who watched or played what.

## Status

The hub is designed, not built. Nothing on this page is available yet, details can still change, and there is no date. v1 of {name} ships without it.
